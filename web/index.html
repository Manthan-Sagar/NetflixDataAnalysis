1. Executive Summary & Core Strategic Takeaways

This audit evaluates the architectural composition, acquisition velocity, and supply-chain concentration of Netflix's global catalog. Leveraging 1NF-normalized relational tables and pure SQL analytics, the analysis surfaces three headline strategic pillars:

Pillar A — The Retention Pivot: While Movies account for 69.7% of cumulative volume, TV Series expanded their share of annual additions from 25.0% in 2018 to 33.7% in 2021. Episodic television serves as the primary defense against monthly churn by sustaining continuous engagement.
Pillar B — Supply Chain Vulnerability: Content production remains excessively centralized: the Top 3 countries (US, India, UK) produce 62.9% of all titles, and the Top 10 control 85.8%, creating significant exposure to regional licensing expiration and foreign quota policies.
Pillar C — The Family Entertainment Deficit: Over 61% of the entire library is rated for mature audiences (TV-MA & TV-14). While this cements Netflix's brand in prestige adult drama, family/preschool programming remains under 20%, leaving a sizable moat for Disney+ and Apple TV+.

2. SQL Analytics Architecture

All business calculations, multi-table joins, and windowed frequency distributions in this project are executed in standard SQL against an optimized SQLite relational schema:

-- 1NF Normalization: Multi-valued fields exploded into junction tables
CREATE TABLE genre_map (show_id TEXT, genre TEXT, PRIMARY KEY (show_id, genre));
CREATE TABLE country_map (show_id TEXT, country TEXT, PRIMARY KEY (show_id, country));

-- Multi-table join aggregation for Top Genres:
SELECT g.genre, COUNT(*) AS total_count,
       SUM(CASE WHEN n.type = 'Movie' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS movie_count,
       SUM(CASE WHEN n.type = 'TV Show' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS tv_count
FROM genre_map g
JOIN netflix_titles n ON g.show_id = n.show_id
GROUP BY g.genre ORDER BY total_count DESC LIMIT 15;

3. Strategic Recommendations for Leadership

  • Mitigate Season 1 Attrition: 67% of TV series cancel after Season 1. Commission 2-season guaranteed pilots for high-engagement IP to build subscriber loyalty.
  • Diversify Beyond Top 3 Hubs: Accelerate studio investments across Latin America, South Korea, and Southeast Asia to hedge licensing concentration risks.
  • Close the Family Moat: Bolster high-replay animated preschool programming to reduce household churn during summer breaks.
